About

Fixer Premiere is your gateway to the world of independent cinema. Integrated within the FIXER app ecosystem, we unite indie directors and independent filmmakers in one space, offering niche movies available exclusively on our platform.

If you're an independent filmmaker, propose your movie for listing on Fixer Premiere and start earning from its views. Regardless of your past successes, as long as your film meets our standards, we'd be delighted to welcome you. Join us and showcase your niche cinema to the world!
