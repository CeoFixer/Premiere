About us

Introducing Fixer Premiere

Fixer Premiere is your gateway to the world of independent cinema. We're the new wave in indie cinema and we're just getting started.

A Unique Ecosystem

Integrated within the FIXER app ecosystem, we strive to showcase undiscovered talent from across the globe. By uniting indie directors and filmmakers in one exclusive platform, we're breaking down traditional barriers and offering a fresh perspective on the cinematic experience.

The Future of Indie Cinema

We celebrate the diversity of storytelling. From LGBTQ+ films to narratives that tackle pressing issues, Fixer Premiere is a haven for filmmakers who push the boundaries. Here, your film’s genre or subject matter isn’t a limitation—it’s an asset.

Uniting Filmmakers from Around the Globe

Be a part of this exciting journey as we redefine the indie film landscape, one unique story at a time.

Launchpad for Filmmakers

Our global reach allows us to bring together filmmakers from diverse backgrounds and cultures. Whether you're from a small town or a bustling city, Fixer Premiere offers an international platform where your voice can be heard, and your stories shared.

Be Seen, Make a Difference

Now, every filmmaker has the right to be seen, irrespective of producers, distributors, or any other industry ties. Your vision matters, and we’re here to make sure it gets the spotlight it deserves.

LA PLAGE

LGBTQ+ Drama 2023 • 18+ • 2h 01 min • Ukraine

LA PLAGE - Ukrainian independent film, anthem of the new wave. Free relationships, non-traditional couples, parental care. Love and infatuation. Loyalty and betrayal.

It is about those who are not stuck in the world of rules and outdated traditions. Heroes break through to their truth through cinematography, music, drugs and sex. Age and gender are not important for them, their closeness is a bright emotional outburst against the background of Normand landscapes. They prove that love is beautiful in any form.

What destroys principles? What makes you look at relations between the sexes from a different angle? An instant feeling or a conscious passion? Parental upbringing or an instinctive impulse? We are witnessing the beginning of a protest that has become a personal revolution.
