Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Short version: we don’t use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies.

What we store on your device

  • Access code (local storage, key fp_access_token) — remembers that you bought a ticket so you can watch without logging in. It stays until you press “Sign out on this device” or clear your browser data.

This storage is strictly necessary for the service you asked for, so it does not require consent.

Third parties

When you pay, you are taken to Stripe’s checkout page, which uses its own cookies for fraud prevention and security — see Stripe’s cookie policy. If a trailer or film is played from an external video provider, that provider may set its own cookies.

Your choices

You can delete stored data at any time in your browser settings. If you do, use your e-mailed link or My access to watch again.
